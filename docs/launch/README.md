## Tone rules (apply across all drafts)
- EN headline: **"Deploy AI agents that don't bankrupt you at 3am."**
- KR headline: **"AI 에이전트, 만들었으면 돌려야죠. 월 $0.01부터."**
- No marketing-speak. State what it does, then give a number.
- Numbers we earned the right to use: `$1.26/month for 7 agents`,
  `113 tests`, `$47,000 cost runaway industry stat`, `88% prod failure stat`.
- Anything Kelly's personal accounts shouldn't say in public stays in the
  blog post draft — review carefully before posting.
